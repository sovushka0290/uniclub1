# Design System Rules

## 1. Visual Theme
Bold Brand Identity, inspired by high-energy educational platforms. Features solid bold header/hero sections, clean white content areas, and large-radius colorful cards.

## 2. Colors
- `brand-primary`: #6366F1 (Indigo - Hero Background)
- `brand-accent`: #FCD34D (Amber/Yellow - Highlights)
- `surface-base`: #FFFFFF (Main content background)
- `text-on-brand`: #FFFFFF (White text on bold backgrounds)
- `text-primary`: #1E293B (Slate-900)
- `text-secondary`: #64748B (Slate-500)

## 3. Typography
- Primary Font: Inter / Geist.
- Hero Title: Massive, bold, white. (72px+ / 800 weight).
- Card Title: Bold, distinct. (28px+ / 700 weight).
- Links: Bold uppercase with arrows.

## 4. Spacing & Layout
- Large solid hero section covering top 40-50% of screen height.
- Main grid using 2-3 columns on desktop.
- Gaps: 24px - 32px between cards.
- Max-width: 1200px for the grid container.

## 5. Components & Effects
- Cards: Solid backgrounds (Indigo, Emerald, Cyan, Rose, Violet), 40px rounded corners.
- Shadows: Soft shadows to provide depth on white background.
- Icons: Integrated into cards, often positioned at the bottom right.
- Buttons: Large, pill-shaped or slightly rounded, high contrast.
